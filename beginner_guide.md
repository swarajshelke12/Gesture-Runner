### Gesture Runner Beginner's Guide

**Getting Started
1. Hardware Requirements**
- A webcam (built-in or external)
- A stable internet connection (only needed for first-time setup)
- Browser with hardware acceleration enabled

2. Initial Setup
1. Open your browser and grant camera permissions when prompted
2. Make sure your hand is visible in the camera frame
3. Check that your index finger is extended toward the camera

**Basic Controls
1. Lane Switching
- Move your index finger LEFT or RIGHT to change lanes
- Keep movements smooth and toward the edges of the screen

2. Jumping
- Raise your index finger ABOVE the gold horizontal line
- Hold for 0.5 seconds to register a jump
- Note: Holding up does NOT repeat the jump

**Gameplay Tips
1. Start with 'Slow' difficulty to learn
2. Focus on lane switching rhythm
3. Watch for obstacles in the next 1-2 blocks ahead
4. Don't panic if you die - the game resets automatically

**Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Try not to move your hand too quickly between directions
- Don't keep your finger raised constantly (wait for the reset
- Avoid reflecting light onto the webcam
